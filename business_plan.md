# DevLights Productization & Business Plan

## Phase 13 - Hardware Productization
### Goals
- Move from breadboard to a custom PCB containing an ESP32-C3 or ESP32-S3 module, power delivery (buck converter if using 12V supply for longer strips), and logic level shifters (3.3V to 5V for reliable WS2812B data).
- Design a custom 3D printable or injection molded enclosure.
- Ensure CE/FCC compliance for EMI.

## Phase 14 - Product Validation
### Pilot Program
- Assemble 20 pilot units.
- Distribute to local developers and gamers.
- Collect metrics via a survey:
  - Ease of setup.
  - Perceived latency in Game Mode.
  - Value proposition (price point willingness).

## Phase 15 - Commercial Product
### SKUs
1. **Starter Kit**: ESP32 Controller, 2M 60LED/m strip, USB C power delivery.
2. **Pro Kit**: Wi-Fi enabled controller, 5M 144LED/m strip, dedicated external power brick, app access.
3. **Multi-Zone Kit**: 3 Controllers syncing over UDP for whole-room lighting.

### Next Steps
- Establish Shopify store.
- Set up manufacturing partner in Shenzhen for PCBA.
- Launch a small Kickstarter to fund the initial batch of 500 units.
